# NBA LOCKBOX Research Framework — 2026–27

## Purpose
Maintain an evidence-based NBA research database. Keep research separate from betting tickets, odds, wagers and results.

## Core Team Metrics
- Pace: possessions per 48 minutes; season and recent trends.
- Offensive rating: points scored per 100 possessions.
- Defensive rating: points allowed per 100 possessions.
- Net rating, opponent efficiency, home/away performance and rest.
- Matchup-specific expected possession environment, labeled as a projection.

## Player Metrics
- Points, rebounds, assists, minutes, field-goal attempts and three-point attempts.
- Last 3, 5, 8, 12 and 20 game trends, with sample sizes.
- Usage, touches, time of possession and shot distribution.
- Potential assists, rebound chances and conversion rates.
- Opponent-specific historical splits, with season labels.

## Matchup Analysis
- Defensive assignments, positional tendencies and coverage schemes.
- Opponent shooting profile, rim protection and rebounding environment.
- Injuries, starting lineups, rotations and with/without teammate splits.
- Pace and efficiency considered together, not independently.

## Data Integrity
- Label sources, collection dates and season type.
- Distinguish verified statistics, calculated metrics and projections.
- Never invent missing statistics or treat absent data as zero.
- Prevent duplicate games and distinguish regular season from playoffs.
- Keep sportsbook odds and ticket records outside the research database.

## Daily Research Output
1. Verify schedule and injury reports.
2. Assess pace, efficiency and game environment.
3. Review player form, minutes, usage and tracking metrics.
4. Evaluate defensive matchups and lineup effects.
5. Rank research opportunities with supporting evidence and risks.
6. Record source coverage and missing data.
